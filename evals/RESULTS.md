## Misses

- **bad-reserve-release** (MISS (known)): 200 generated cases with seed 1 do not find it: the bug needs a partial fill and then a cancel of the same buy order, and the generator rarely produces both. With 500 cases and seed 1 the invariants find it, and with 5000 cases seeds 1 to 3 all find it. The check count in gate.yaml is a cost and coverage trade-off owned by a human; it is not raised to fit this case.
- **bad-runtime-secret** (MISS (known)): No security check exists yet (Phase 5): nothing scans for secrets, so the gate passes the change. The token is assembled from pieces, so a scanner that only matches literals would miss it too.
